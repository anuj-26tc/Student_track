🎓 StudentTrack

Academic progress and practical skills, together.

A unified student growth platform. Teachers maintain official subject marks and attendance, while students build a record of their technical skills, learning goals and practical projects.

  • 📊 Track subject-wise marks and academic progress
  • 📅 Monitor subject-wise attendance
  • 💡 Record skills, proficiency and learning progress
  • 🧩 Showcase projects and technologies used
  • 🎯 Set learning goals and milestones
